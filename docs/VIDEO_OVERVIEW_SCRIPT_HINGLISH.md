# Warehouse Intelligence — Video Overview Script (Hinglish)

**Suggested duration:** 1 minute 45 seconds to 2 minutes 15 seconds  
**Style:** Natural walkthrough. Isse word-to-word padhne ki zaroorat nahi; normal conversation ki tarah bolo.

## Recording flow

### 0:00–0:12 | Intro — Command Center screen

"Hey everyone! Aaj main apna project, Warehouse Intelligence, share kar raha hoon. Ye ek warehouse operations dashboard hai jahan inventory, storage capacity aur warehouse activities ko ek hi place par analyse kar sakte hain."

### 0:12–0:28 | Command Center — KPI cards aur charts

"Sabse pehle Command Center hai. Yahan total stock units, inventory value, active SKUs, capacity utilization aur low-stock products ka quick overview milta hai. Neeche warehouse-wise inventory value, category mix aur inventory ageing ke charts hain. Filters change karne par analysis bhi update hota hai."

### 0:28–0:48 | Inventory Intelligence — table aur ABC chart

"Inventory Intelligence section mein main stock health ko detail mein dekh sakta hoon. Yahan low stock, out of stock, slow-moving inventory aur ABC classification available hai. Isse identify karna easy hota hai ki kaunse products par replenishment ya inventory review ki zaroorat hai."

### 0:48–1:12 | 2D Floor Plan aur 3D Digital Twin

"Project ka ek interesting part iska warehouse layout hai. 2D Floor Plan mein racks ko top view se dekh sakte hain. Kisi rack par click karne se uski capacity, occupied space, utilization aur stored SKUs ki details milti hain.

Same selection 3D view ke saath linked hai. 3D scene ko rotate, zoom aur pan kar sakte hain, rack select kar sakte hain aur occupancy heatmap dekh sakte hain."

### 1:12–1:28 | Workforce & Equipment / Operations

"Workforce and Equipment section mein receiving, replenishment aur picking-to-dispatch ke operating scenarios explore kar sakte hain. Iske saath inbound aur outbound pages receiving aur order fulfilment ko analyse karte hain."

### 1:28–1:43 | Risk & Exceptions / Insights

"Risk and Exceptions section stock aur capacity issues ko highlight karta hai. Insights section current data ke basis par important observations aur recommendations deta hai."

### 1:43–1:58 | Data Explorer / CSV upload

"Ek aur useful feature CSV upload hai. User apni inventory file upload karke columns map kar sakta hai aur dashboard ko apne data ke saath explore kar sakta hai. Reports ko CSV format mein download bhi kar sakte hain."

### 1:58–2:10 | Closing — back to Command Center

"Is project mein maine Python, Streamlit, Pandas, NumPy, Plotly, JavaScript aur Three.js use kiya hai. Mera focus tha warehouse data ko useful operational insights aur interactive visualization mein convert karna. Thanks for watching!"

## Quick recording checklist

- Browser ko full screen karo aur bookmarks/address bar hide kar do.
- Start Command Center par karo; page load hone ke baad hi recording shuru karo.
- 2D Floor Plan mein ek rack click karke detail panel dikhana.
- 3D view mein mouse drag karke model rotate aur scroll karke zoom dikhana.
- Inventory table mein 2–3 rows scroll karna.
- CSV upload ka screen dikhana ho toh sample file use karo; apna personal data mat dikhana.
- Demo dataset use kar rahe ho toh video mein ise sample/demo data kehna.
- Jo feature screen par actually kaam karta hai, wahi narration mein mention karna.
